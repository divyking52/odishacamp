Good to know

FAQ

Where do we meet?
Most day adventures start at Godibari Nature Camp, Chandaka. We’ll drop the pin and timing on WhatsApp after you enquire.
What ages can join?
Families and mixed groups are welcome. Climb and skywalk minimums depend on height/comfort — tell us ages when you book and we’ll tailor the mix.
Do you provide gear?
Yes — kayaks, life jackets, climb kit, and camp basics. Wear comfortable clothes, shoes you can get wet, and bring sun protection.
How do payments work?
Confirm on WhatsApp. We’ll share the package total and payment steps for your date. Group discounts available for larger crews.
What if it rains?
Light rain is often still runnable. For heavy weather we reschedule — your safety leads the call.
